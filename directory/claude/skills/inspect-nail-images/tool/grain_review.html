Wood grain test · mark the visible evidence

This test measures changes in local edge direction and texture around the head. It does not directly identify crushed fibres, cracks or physical nail height. No trained model, PN threshold or production change.

Test results and limitations

The primary score averages the two largest increases in off-grain gradient energy among at least six usable sections. The reference grain direction comes only from nearby wood in the same section. Higher scores mean more local directional disruption, not confirmed indentation. All seven one-pixel head-boundary variants remain in the comparison; ranges show boundary sensitivity.

These are reused examples. Group, lighting, camera and native head-size differences can confound the result. One physical source group of raised nails cannot establish performance on other pallets. No absence-of-texture or missing measurement is interpreted as a raised nail.

Mark the wood you can actually see

Choose a mark type, then drag a short stroke directly over the sign on the left image. Keep it narrow: mark the rim or crushed fibres, not the entire nail. Use “Intact grain” for clearly continuous wood, or “Head boundary” if the yellow outline is wrong. “Insufficient detail” is a valid review outcome.

Marks stay in this browser. When finished, download the marks as a backup and tell me you are done. Sources and existing labels are unchanged; nothing is uploaded.
